## Core diagnosis

The shop currently mixes three very different merchandising systems:
1. kawaii stickers/apparel;
2. motivational/wellness wall art;
3. printable planners, trackers and small-business forms.

That is not inherently an Etsy search penalty, but it weakens storefront coherence, featured-item strategy, shop sections, visual branding and cross-sell logic.

The one proven sale is physical: the coffee-bean sticker. Do not remove the kawaii line blindly.

## Pricing direction

Current digital singles are mostly $5.99-$12.99. Strong printable competitors commonly use lower entry prices and bundles to raise order value.

Recommended test structure:
- simple single-sheet / short printable: $2.99-$4.99
- deeper focused planner: $4.99-$7.99
- niche business kit / forms bundle: $8.99-$14.99
- category bundle: $12.99-$19.99
- whole printable shop bundle only after quality is consistent: $19.99-$29.99

Do not blanket-discount everything. Use controlled tests by product family.

## Thumbnail system

### Printables
Image 1:
- product name in 3-5 words;
- clear mockup showing actual pages;
- one visual badge: “Instant PDF” / “XX pages” / “A4 + Letter” only when true.

Images 2-5:
- page spread;
- close-up;
- “what’s included” grid;
- size/file-format card;
- use-case mockup.

### Stickers
Image 1:
- oversized sticker on clean high-contrast background;
- no tiny multi-prop scene;
- character occupies most of the frame.

Images 2-5:
- laptop/tumbler scale;
- hand/size reference;
- finish/material close-up;
- collection cross-sell.

## Listing-copy rules

Titles:
- front-load exact buyer query;
- avoid redundant keyword stuffing;
- put use-case modifiers after the core phrase.

Descriptions:
1. buyer outcome;
2. what is included;
3. file/material specs;
4. how delivery/use works;
5. important limitations;
6. FAQ.

Tags:
- use all available tag slots;
- cover buyer, use case, product type, occasion and audience;
- avoid repeating the exact same phrase across every slot.

## Policy cleanup

Current privacy-policy text contains an accidental assistant-style sentence:
“That’s your Privacy policy done — and with it, your entire shop-policy section ...”

Remove that sentence from the live policy.

The cancellation policy is “not accepted.” For digital goods, keep wording aligned with Etsy’s instant-download rules and add a support-first message for file issues.

## Success metrics

Track per listing:
- impressions
- visits
- favorites
- add-to-cart
- orders
- conversion rate
- revenue
- Etsy fees
- POD cost/shipping where applicable
- verified net profit

A listing is not a winner because it gets views. Promote based on orders and contribution profit.
