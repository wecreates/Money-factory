## Product rules
- Etsy remains digital-only.
- POD uses original CYZOR artwork only.
- Never reuse protected competitor artwork/trademarks/characters.
- Reverse-engineer market demand, product selection, price bands, keyword structure and merchandising mechanics—not protected expression.
- Adapt files for each platform's print area, resolution, color space and safe-zone requirements.
- Never push one generic mockup/listing everywhere.
- Marketplace-specific SEO and thumbnail systems.
- Track verified orders, fees, fulfillment cost, returns and contribution profit by marketplace.
