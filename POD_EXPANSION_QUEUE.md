# POD Expansion Queue — Start Only After Etsy Completion

Owner directive:
After the Etsy digital-only rebuild is fully live and verified, expand original CYZOR designs to POD marketplaces. Etsy is explicitly excluded from POD.

## Start gate
Do not begin POD publishing until:
- all 59 Etsy listings are live as intended;
- Etsy public QA passes;
- digital-file delivery is verified;
- no old POD Etsy listing remains.

## Marketplace research queue
Evaluate and prioritize:
1. Redbubble
2. TeePublic
3. Zazzle
4. Spreadshirt / Spreadshop
5. Spring
6. Society6
7. Fine Art America
8. Threadless
9. Design By Humans
10. Fourthwall
11. Amazon Merch on Demand, if account access is available
12. Other credible POD storefronts that permit original uploaded designs

## Product rules
- Etsy remains digital-only.
- POD uses original CYZOR artwork only.
- Never reuse protected competitor artwork/trademarks/characters.
- Reverse-engineer market demand, product selection, price bands, keyword structure and merchandising mechanics—not protected expression.
- Adapt files for each platform's print area, resolution, color space and safe-zone requirements.
- Never push one generic mockup/listing everywhere.
- Marketplace-specific SEO and thumbnail systems.
- Track verified orders, fees, fulfillment cost, returns and contribution profit by marketplace.
